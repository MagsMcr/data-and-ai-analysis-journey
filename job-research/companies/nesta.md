NESTA — Company Profile
What it is: The UK's innovation agency for social good. They design, test and scale solutions to society's biggest problems, with three core missions: give every child a fair start, help people live healthy lives, and create a sustainable future. They work in three roles: as an innovation partner working with frontline organisations, as a venture builder supporting early-stage businesses, and as a system shaper creating conditions for innovation. Institute for Fiscal Studies
Structure: Independent foundation but closely connected to government — they run major programmes in partnership with the Department for Education Nesta, which gives them real influence without being a government body. This is exactly the type of organisation you recognised from fashion — credible, funded, impactful, with one eye on policy at all times.
